# Core Business Rules

1. Every submitted order gets a unique Order ID.
2. A customer sees only their own orders, except through a secure tracking link that deliberately shows less.
3. An order isn't confirmed until an authorised admin confirms it.
4. The estimate isn't the final quote until the press confirms it.
5. Production doesn't begin before the order is confirmed and the required artwork is approved.
6. Every important status change gets a timestamp.
7. A modification request includes a reason or instructions.
8. Rejected and cancelled orders stay in the records.
9. Payment status is independent of production status.
10. A payment counts as successful only after trusted server-side verification or authorised manual confirmation.
11. Customer tracking never reveals internal notes or private information.
12. Only authorised roles can update production stages.
13. The deployed QR points to the actual live hosting URL.
14. Demo or simulated data is labelled as demo data.
