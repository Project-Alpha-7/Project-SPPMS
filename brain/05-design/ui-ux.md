# UI/UX Direction

## Overall
A professional print-industry SaaS interface: clean, confident, functional and easy to understand.

## Blueprint palette (suggested)
- Deep navy: navigation and primary structure
- White / light grey: content surfaces
- Cyan/blue accent: primary actions and active states
- Green: completed/success
- Amber: pending/attention
- Red: errors, rejections, critical issues

> The imported Stitch design system uses a CMYK-accent "Industrial Editorial Prepress" identity
> instead. See [design-system.md](design-system.md). Which one wins is an open question.

## Responsive requirements
- Customer ordering works well on mobile.
- The QR landing and tracking pages are mobile-friendly.
- The admin dashboard works on desktop and tablet.
- Tables adapt to small screens without hiding essential actions.
- Forms have clear labels, validation and error messages.

## Screens to design (Stage 2)
Customer: landing · catalogue · configurator · upload · checkout/delivery · confirmation · tracking · order history.
Admin: dashboard · order list · order detail · production timeline · job card · payments · inventory · reports.
